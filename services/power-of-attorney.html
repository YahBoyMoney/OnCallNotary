Power of Attorney Notary Services

Professional notarization of Power of Attorney documents throughout San Bernardino County. We come to your location for convenient service.

Call (909) 751-8439 Schedule Appointment

Types of Power of Attorney We Notarize

General Power of Attorney

Broad authority to act on your behalf in various matters.

Durable Power of Attorney

Remains valid even if you become incapacitated.

Medical Power of Attorney

Healthcare decisions and medical treatment authorization.

Financial Power of Attorney

Financial transactions and banking matters.

Why Choose Our Power of Attorney Notary Services?

  • Experienced with all types of POA documents
  • Mobile service - we come to you at home, jail, hospital, or office
  • Available 24/7 for urgent POA notarizations
  • Proper execution according to California law
  • Fast, professional service with attention to detail

What to Prepare for Your POA Notarization

  • Completed Power of Attorney document (unsigned)
  • Valid government-issued photo ID for the principal (person granting power)
  • Agent's information (person receiving power) if present
  • Payment method (cash, card, Venmo, or Zelle)
